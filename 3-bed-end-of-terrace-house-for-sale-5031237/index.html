About the property

Description property

9 Castleview Drive is a modern three-bedroom end-of-terrace house offering spacious living accommodation with rear garden and side access. Presented in good condition throughout, the property benefits from renovated bathrooms, natural gas central heating and double-glazed PVC windows. Communal on-street car parking

The ground floor comprises an entrance hall with guest w.c., living room and kitchen/dining room. Upstairs, there are three bedrooms, including a main bedroom with an en-suite shower room, together with a family bathroom. The rear garden provides generous outdoor space complemented by convenient side access, and barna shed.

Nearby Applewood Village offers a convenient range of everyday amenities, including EUROSPAR, Look Mam No Hands café and Applewood Medical Centre. Local schools include Thornleigh Educate Together National School, while Applewood Community Centre provides sports facilities and a range of classes and activities.

Swords town centre offers further amenities, with extensive shopping, dining and cinema facilities at the Pavilions Shopping Centre. Swords Express provides connections between Swords and Dublin city centre via the Port Tunnel.
